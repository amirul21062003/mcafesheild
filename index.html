Frequently Asked

Common Questions

Quick answers to the most common McAfee support queries.

How do I install McAfee on a new computer? +
To install McAfee on a new computer, you need your product activation key or the email address associated with your McAfee account. Visit the McAfee website, sign in, and download the installer. Run the downloaded file and follow the on-screen prompts. If you run into issues, our support team can guide you through the entire process remotely.
My McAfee subscription expired — how do I renew it? +
You can renew your McAfee subscription directly through the McAfee application on your computer, or by logging into your McAfee account online. If you're having trouble with the renewal process, payment errors, or auto-renewal settings, call our support line and a technician will walk you through it step by step.
McAfee is slowing down my computer — what can I do? +
Performance slowdowns are often caused by full system scans running in the background. You can schedule scans for off-peak hours, adjust real-time scanning settings, or add trusted programs to the exclusion list. Our technicians can remotely optimize your McAfee settings to minimize performance impact without reducing protection.
How do I activate McAfee with a product key? +
Open McAfee on your computer and look for the "My Account" or "Subscription" section. Click "Enter Product Key" and type your 25-character activation code. Alternatively, visit mcafee.com/activate in your browser and enter the key there. If your key isn't being accepted or you receive an error, contact our support team for immediate assistance.
Is this an official McAfee support service? +
No — this is an independent, third-party technical support service and is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to McAfee LLC or any of its subsidiaries. McAfee® is a registered trademark of McAfee LLC. For official first-party support, please visit mcafee.com. We provide independent technical assistance and support charges may apply.
How do I uninstall and reinstall McAfee? +
McAfee provides a dedicated removal tool called MCPR (McAfee Consumer Product Removal) that cleanly uninstalls all McAfee components. Download it from the McAfee website, run it, and restart your computer. Then reinstall from your account portal. Our technicians can guide you through this process if you encounter any problems during removal or reinstallation.
